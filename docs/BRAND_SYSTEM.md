# Vanta Helix Brand System

## Core idea

**Intelligence, made instinctive.**

Vanta should feel like a future consumer-technology brand with the restraint of a high-end industrial-design studio and the editorial confidence of a research publication.

## Palette

- Obsidian: `#050409`
- Black-violet panel: `#0B0911`
- Warm editorial stone: `#E8E5DF`
- Ultraviolet: `#9B75FF`
- Pale violet: `#D7CAFF`
- Ice cyan: `#6FE6FF`
- Pale cyan: `#B8F5FF`
- Silver: `#C7CED8`

Ultraviolet is the primary Vanta signal. Cyan is a secondary system/feedback color rather than the entire identity.

## Image hierarchy

1. **Photorealistic product / on-body studies** lead the public product story.
2. **Human testing and environment scenes** demonstrate context and scale.
3. **Macro/cutaway studies** communicate material, sensor, and local-compute concepts.
4. **Technical SVG diagrams** explain architecture, privacy, and signal flow.

This restores the original site's cinematic product-demo character while retaining the newer application's technical clarity.

## Motion

Use motion only to imply signal, calibration, or environment response:

- subtle pointer parallax
- scan-line overlays
- slow orbital geometry
- signal travel
- mode transitions

All ornamental motion is disabled when reduced motion is requested.

## Claims

Always label photorealistic hardware imagery as concept visualization. Do not present renders as a commercially shipping Vanta One unit.
